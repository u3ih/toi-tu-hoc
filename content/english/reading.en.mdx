title: Reading in English

Reading is the cheapest way to grow your vocabulary. But read the wrong way and it is exhausting —
I abandoned a lot of books for exactly that reason.

## Two modes of reading

My biggest mistake was mixing these two together.

**Extensive reading** — read a lot, look up very little, favour volume. This is about 80% of my
reading time now.

**Intensive reading** — take one short passage, look up every unknown word, make Anki cards from it.
This is where my card material comes from.

<Callout type="tip" title="Decide before each session">
Wanting to read fast *and* stopping for every word is a guaranteed recipe for giving up.

Before I open the book, I say it out loud: today is extensive, or today is intensive.
</Callout>

## How I pick books

1. **Start with a genre you have read a lot of in your own language.** You already know the tropes,
   so your brain only has to handle the language.
2. **Favour long series.** By book two the author reuses the same vocabulary. You get free review
   without doing anything.
3. **Avoid the classics at first.** I tried and quit after two chapters. Archaic words and long
   sentences do nothing for everyday English.

Some easy ways in: young-adult fiction, long-running web serials, non-fiction about a field you
already know well.

## Lookups have to be fast

I installed [Ddict](/english/ddict-setup/) so I can look a word up by double-clicking it, right on
the page I am reading.

It sounds trivial, but the difference between "1 second to look up" and "15 seconds to look up"
decides whether I read 5 pages that day or 50.

<Callout type="story">
Before I had a fast lookup tool, I read on my laptop with a dictionary tab open. Every unfamiliar
word meant select, switch tab, paste, read, switch back.

I measured it once: in a 45-minute evening, I read 3 pages.

After installing a lookup extension, the same 45 minutes got me about 20 pages. I had not got any
better. I was just no longer being interrupted.
</Callout>

## How I measure progress

Not by feel — feel always tells you that you are getting nowhere. I measure three numbers:

- Pages read per week.
- Lookups per page — this one should fall month over month.
- Can I keep reading **without translating in my head**?

The third is the most important, and the last one to arrive.
